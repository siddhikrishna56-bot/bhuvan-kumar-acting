Training Philosophy

The Power of Pure 1-on-1 Mentorship

Acting cannot be learned effectively in crowded rooms. Real screen acting demands individualized attention, bespoke diagnostic feedback, and intensive one-on-one camera execution tailored specifically to your personality.

01

Technical Execution & Camera Mastery

Screen acting is intensely precise. You work directly with the camera to master lighting awareness, lens focal depth, subtle mic projection, and shot continuity.

  • 100% solo camera time — every take is analyzed on screen with you
  • Intimate audio voice modulation and dialogue delivery rhythm
  • Real set blocking, eye-line discipline, and frame awareness
  • Eliminating artificial mannerisms through private mirror coaching
02

Subconscious Sensitivity & Emotional Truth

Enriching your internal actor through personalized psychological exercises that expand your imagination, build unshakeable self-esteem, and tap deep emotional memories.

  • Subconscious mental conditioning and confidence building
  • Emotional memory recall to trigger genuine tears, joy, and tension
  • Spontaneous improvisation without fear of judgment
  • Discovering your authentic personal style and screen identity
Zero Group Compromises

Strictly 1-on-1 Personal Training

We do not conduct crowd training. In our studio, 100% of the mentor's time, the camera lens, and the technical setup belong solely to you. You get direct coaching, instantaneous playback reviews, and tailored guidance that accelerates your growth tenfold.

Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you choose the right acting training path in Mumbai.

Why is the training conducted strictly 1-on-1?

Screen acting is an intensely intimate and personal craft. In mass classrooms, students get mere minutes in front of the lens. Our 1-on-1 personal training format guarantees that 100% of the mentor's attention, the camera equipment, and the playback critique are dedicated exclusively to your growth every session.

What is the structure of the 9-Month 1-on-1 Acting Course?

The Full Acting Course (Basic & Advance) spans 36 private sessions over 9 months. Sessions are held once a week (4 sessions in a month), with each session lasting 2 hours of direct 1-on-1 mentorship. The fee is ₹2,00,000/- (₹2 Lakhs).

Do you offer short-term or specialized 1-on-1 sessions?

Yes. We offer a dedicated (1 Month) Certificate Course of 4 intensive private sessions held on Sundays (2 hours each) for ₹25,000/-. In addition, specialized private master sessions for Diction, Character Training, Portfolio Training, and Shoot Location Training are available at ₹5,000/- per hour.

Do I need prior experience to begin 1-on-1 training?

No prior experience is necessary. Because training is strictly 1-on-1, the curriculum is customized to your exact starting baseline — whether you are a complete beginner seeking self-confidence and speech clarity, or a working actor seeking polish for upcoming audition roles.

How are students promoted for TV, Films, and Commercials?

Our institute is situated in Gokuldham Colony, Goregaon East, Mumbai. We maintain active connections with leading producers, directors, and choreographers. Candidates demonstrating high merit and camera readiness are awarded certification and directly recommended to casting directors.

What payment methods are accepted?

Fees must be paid in advance at the time of admission via Online Bank Transfer (NEFT/IMPS/UPI) or Cheque drawn in favour of Bhuvan Kumar. Please note that fees are strictly non-refundable under any circumstances.
